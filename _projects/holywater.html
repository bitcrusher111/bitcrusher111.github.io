--- title: holy water (2024) category: works date: 2024-11-08 image: "/assets/images/works/galleryicons/soloworks/holywater.jpg" permalink: "/works/holywater/" videos: - https://www.youtube.com/embed/rcfLqninbxQ credits: - performance, Liza Kereselidze - composition, Candid Rütter - video/lights, Candid Rütter - costume, Liza Kereselidze, Candid Rütter - make-up, Liza Kereselidze, Marco Merenda gallery: - "/assets/images/works/soloworks/holywater/hw_1.jpg" - "/assets/images/works/soloworks/holywater/hw_2.jpg" - "/assets/images/works/soloworks/holywater/hw_3.jpg" - "/assets/images/works/soloworks/holywater/hw_4.jpg" - "/assets/images/works/soloworks/holywater/hw_5.jpg" - "/assets/images/works/soloworks/holywater/hw_6.png" photo_credits: Photos by Swen Wied --- Water is the foundation of all life, yet it is also an immensely destructive force. It shapes the living and the non-living alike. It permeates matter and mind, flowing with quiet persistence as it gradually carves its way forward. Water is a hyperobject: we can perceive only fragments of its totality. HOLY WATER is a speculative, multimedia ritual that approaches the concept of the hyperobject in an atmospheric and associative manner. In a world in which nature is often treated as a stockpile of resources, HOLY WATER seeks to explore alternative frameworks for relating to the natural world—and, in doing so, to transform our relationship to it. Using contact microphones attached to a vessel filled with water, the performer is able to set the water itself into vibration. Inside the vessel, a hydrophone captures and amplifies the sounds produced within the water, creating a feedback loop between the performer and the water vessel.
