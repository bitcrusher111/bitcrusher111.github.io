It works on the identifications of the imagined other, the desiring and adoring, but also the absent, which is reinterpreted through its own power. 'we three we three i bend for the sake of the existence of us we three in what kind of common we does the I remain - can the I still remain? must perhaps even the I remain so that the we becomes all the more possible? I I I accept my I, me so that I, you, your you, your 'I', can also accept it create space together for the one yours, your you, mine too ...'
