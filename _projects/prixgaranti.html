--- title: PRIX GARANTI category: collectives date: 2024-01-03 image: "/assets/images/works/galleryicons/ongoingprojects/prixgaranti.webp" permalink: "/collectives/prixgaranti/" videos: - https://www.youtube.com/embed/uV3P9G8OLOM - https://www.youtube.com/embed/_1BIxUK4-gw links: - label: Linktree url: https://linktr.ee/prixgaranti --- We are the voice of our friends, and our friends are the friends of many. In the face of global events, we believe in local solidarity, in the power of art, lyrics, cohesion, shared feelings, and narratives. Our community is very large and very small. We make music for and against it, we shape dreams, sometimes hymns, accompanying people through summer, winter, and sometimes on the highway. Style is a means to an end, and we serve and live the stories we sell ourselves.
