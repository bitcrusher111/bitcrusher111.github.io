While the heat robs us of sleep, the world itself seems to be in turmoil. How do we navigate these changing conditions? In an atmospheric evening of theatre, ultrablau explores the feeling when the ground beneath our feet begins to shake. The theatre piece Ultrablau is the first joint work by Kaija Knauer, Selina Pfeiffer and Jonas Bernetta. From their starting point, their interest in strategies for staging and making the climate crisis tangible, they are working with performative, documentary and multimedia approaches in search of a form that could perhaps be described as a theatre essay.
